# Battle artwork sources

These backgrounds use web-optimized reproductions of public-domain medieval
manuscript illustrations. The set emphasizes armored formations, banners, and
army-versus-army combat rather than recognizable individual faces. Delivery
assets are capped at 1,600 pixels and encoded as compressed JPEGs.

- `home.jpg` — [Battle of Agincourt](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schlacht_von_Azincourt.jpg), 15th-century miniature.
- `play.jpg` — [Battle of Crécy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle_of_crecy_froissart.jpg), Loyset Liédet, 15th century.
- `custom-games.jpg` — [Battle of Sluys](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BattleofSluys.jpeg), Loyset Liédet, 15th-century miniature from Froissart's *Chronicles*.
- `match-history.jpg` — [Battle of Poitiers](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle-poitiers(1356).jpg), from Froissart's *Chronicles*, circa 1470–1480.
- `leaderboard.jpg` — [Battle of Montiel](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle_of_Montiel.jpg), Loyset Liédet, 15th century.
- `profile.jpg` — [Battle of Crécy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle_of_Cr%C3%A9cy_-_Grandes_Chroniques_de_France_(c.1415),_f.152v_-_BL_Cotton_MS_Nero_E_II.jpg), from the *Grandes Chroniques de France*, circa 1415.
- `profile-page.jpg` — [Battle of Cresson](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle_of_Cresson.jpg), Jean Colombe, circa 1474.
- `settings.jpg` — [Battle of La Roche-Derrien](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle_of_La_Roche-Derrien.jpg), from Froissart's *Chronicles*, 15th century.

- `weekly.jpg` — [King Sigismund holding council in his tent on the battlefield](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_King_of_Hungary_holding_council_in_his_tent_on_the_battlefield_-_Froissart%27s_Chronicles_%28Volume_IV%2C_part_2%29_%281470-1475%29%2C_f.84_-_BL_Harley_MS_4380.jpg), from Froissart's *Chronicles*, Bruges, circa 1470–1475; British Library Harley MS 4380, f.84, released under CC0.
- `weekly-background.jpg` — [Battle of Nicopolis](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nicopol_final_battle_1398.jpg), from Froissart's *Chronicles*, Bruges, 15th century; Bibliothèque nationale de France MS Fr. 2646, fol. 220, public domain.

The Wikimedia Commons pages identify the underlying works and their faithful
reproductions as public domain.
