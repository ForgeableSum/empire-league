# Public-domain battle artwork

These background images are reproductions of public-domain artwork sourced from Wikimedia Commons.

- `home.jpg` — [Crusaders attack Constantinople](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Crusaders_attack_Constantinople.jpg), unknown artist, circa 1330.
- `play.jpg` — [Battle of Agincourt, St. Alban's Chronicle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Agincour.JPG), unknown artist, circa 1422.
- `match-history.jpg` — [Battle of Nicopolis](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nicopol_final_battle_1398.jpg), from Jean Froissart's *Chroniques*, 15th century.
- `leaderboard.jpg` — [Battle of Tannenberg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battle_of_Tannenberg.jpg), Diebold Schilling the Elder, 15th century.
- `profile.jpg` — [Siege of a city](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Siege_of_a_city,_medieval_miniature.jpg), unknown artist, 1496.
- `settings.jpg` — [Siege of Constantinople](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Siege_constantinople_bnf_fr2691.jpg), 15th-century manuscript miniature.

The Wikimedia Commons file pages mark these works and their faithful reproductions as public domain.
